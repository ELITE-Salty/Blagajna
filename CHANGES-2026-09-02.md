# Changes — clearer Blagajna hierarchy and separate imports

## Blagajna hierarchy
- The main Blagajna selector now groups internal cash desks below their global/main cash desk.
- Selecting a global/main cash desk shows the combined balance and clickable child cash desks.
- Selecting an internal cash desk shows its parent and a one-click `POGLEJ SKUPAJ` button.
- Settings now shows the hierarchy as a tree instead of a flat list.
- Existing document, closing, reporting, balance and sync behavior is preserved.

## Separate imports
- `Zaposleni -> Uvoz zaposlenih CSV` remains the employee import. It only adds/updates employees.
- `Blagajna -> Uvoz akontacij (Excel/CSV)` is a separate import. It matches existing employees and creates BI documents.
- The akontacije import retains the >500 EUR split, <300 EUR parts, 5 EUR rounding and 20th-to-16th date-window rules.
- Added `akontacije-import-vzorec.csv`; the previous `izplacila-import-vzorec.csv` is retained for compatibility.

## Internal transfers
- Added a dedicated `↔ Interni prenos` flow between active internal cash desks under the same global/main Blagajna.
- Transfers are their own transaction type: they do not create BP/BI documents and do not consume BP/BI numbering.
- A transfer decreases the source balance and increases the destination balance; a transfer between sibling desks leaves the parent/global total unchanged.
- Transfers are visible from both source and destination cash desks with date, time, amount, direction, note and sync/lock status.
- Source-funds and month-close checks are enforced before saving or deleting a transfer.
- Reports include a separate internal-transfer ledger and CSV export; the printed Blagajniška knjiga includes `PRENOS` rows so its running saldo reconciles.
- Transfers participate in local/server synchronization and use normalized `cash_transfers` storage in SQLite/PostgreSQL.
- Existing BP/BI entry, employee import, akontacije import, certificates, closing and reporting behavior remains in place.



## v4 – vidnost internega prenosa
- Gumb **↔ Interni prenos** je zdaj jasno prikazan tudi neposredno v modrem bloku *Hierarhija blagajne* pri interni/globalni blagajni z vsaj dvema aktivnima internima lokacijama.
- Gumb ostaja tudi v vrstici akcij nad tabelo dokumentov.
- Dodana je oznaka **v4 · interni prenosi**, da je takoj vidno, ali brskalnik/deployment uporablja novo verzijo frontenda.
- Pogoji za prikaz uporabljajo isti seznam aktivnih internih blagajn kot modal za prenos.

## Akontacije import update
- Zneski do vključno 700 EUR ostanejo en BI.
- Zneski nad 700 EUR se razdelijo na dele med 300 in 500 EUR.
- Vsota delov je vedno natancno enaka izvornemu znesku; uvoz ne spreminja skupnega zneska z zaokrozevanjem.
- Razdeljeni deli se razporedijo na vec razlicnih dni v oknu 20. v mesecu do 16. naslednjega meseca in dobijo razlicne ure.
- Meje dopust listov ostanejo prednostni datumi; dodatni deli se razprsijo po veljavnem oknu.
- Predogled uvoza prikaze tudi uro vsakega BI.
- Odstranjene so vidne razvojne oznake; gumb za interni prenos ostaja nespremenjen in viden.
